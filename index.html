Reform the North

Canada needs reform.
It’s not too late.

The Liberal Party and the Conservative Party have taken this country apart. They put foreigners and foreign wars ahead of the nation, and ahead of the people who built it.

Domestic

Canadians already here are being replaced. Work, shops, and housing go to a workforce imported for the purpose. Temporary labour is treated as a right. The people who pay for the country wait.

Federal and provincial governments now answer to foreign constituencies before they answer to this one. That is not Canada.

Abroad

Since 2022 Canada has sent more than $26 billion to Ukraine. It put $5 billion into a G7 loan for Kyiv and signed a 100-year partnership this month.

The house here is not in order. The chequebook is open anyway.

  • $26 billion to Ukraine

    about 37,000 homes at $700,000

  • $8.5 billion in arms

    a year’s pay for about 24,000 family doctors

  • $5 billion G7 loan

    about 7,000 homes, or a large hospital

  • 100-year partnership

    a century of this country’s credit tied to another war
