CANADA FIRST ALWAYS

Canada needs to reclaim its heritage — the language, law, and culture that made this country work. Borders, housing, and the national chequebook are not optional.

01

Heritage before slogan

“Diversity is our strength” is the biggest lie sold to this country.

Strength comes from a shared culture people join — language, law, memory, and the habits that make strangers into neighbours. A slogan that denies a core culture does not enrich the country. It hollows it. Canada was built on something real. Keep that, and newcomers have something worth joining.

02

Assimilation is the line

Those who come here and refuse Western norms — our laws, our language, our civic life — are asking to live beside Canada, not in it.

Settlement means entering the life of the country: speech, schools, work, and the rules everyone else lives under. Parallel societies are not “diversity.” They are a quiet refusal to become Canadian. The line is clear — join the culture that carries this place, or do not expect it to bend around you.

03

Remigration where needed

Remigration for those who will not assimilate is not cruelty. It is how a country stays itself.

A nation that will not enforce its own culture eventually loses it. Canada first means the people who built and keep this place come before those who reject it. Remigration is the sober end of that principle — for those who refuse the deal, not for those who keep it.

04

Build Canadian capacity

Lean on our US neighbours while we rebuild our own infrastructure.

Rebuild here first — energy, industry, housing, the spine of a country that can stand on its own feet. The United States is not the enemy of Canadian interest. Cutting every tie is not strength; it is theatre. Canada’s interest is a strong home, and allies that help us keep it while we build.
